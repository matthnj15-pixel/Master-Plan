# Roadmap

Priorities are Nate's call. This list proposes an order.

## Next
1. **Balance pass on safety and affordability.** Play several runs and tune the numbers in `src/engine.js` so neither meter is trivially high or a guaranteed drain.
2. **Term two content.** New proposals for years 5 to 8 so the agenda doesn't go empty after the first election.
3. **Opposition mode.** After an election loss, play from one council seat, block or bargain with Greg Marsh's agenda, and run again in four years.

## Later
- **Term limit and successor.** After two terms, groom a successor who inherits approval, the rival, and unfinished projects.
- **Generated proposals.** A pool of proposal templates that appear based on the town's state, so later years don't need hand-written schedules.
- **Milestones.** A goal track (a "comprehensive plan" book) that unlocks new options as the town grows.
- **More towns.** Other Pennsylvania starting towns with different geography and pressures.

## Known gaps
- AI characters only work in the claude.ai build. The GitHub Pages build uses quick options.
- Years after the first election currently have no scheduled proposals besides your own initiatives.
