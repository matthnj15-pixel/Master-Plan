### Suggested fixture fact envelope

Each authored case provides stable IDs for `case`, `actor`, `site`, `option` and evidence. Each fact has a provenance tag from `simulated_fact | scenario_authored_rule | character_belief | flavor`. Hard authority and eligibility checks accept only the applicable factual/rule categories. Relevant recorded history and relationship are bounded inputs, not arbitrary chat transcripts.

`optionId` selects a trusted fixture option with exact site geometry, supported physical plan fields, and explicit authored operational facts. The model cannot fabricate a new route, ownership record, easement or sales policy by writing arbitrary metadata. An option change is a recognized proposed game action; its future confirmed application is what changes the recorded fixture facts. Previewing the option is still private. The pure stance evaluator uses the facts and actor preferences to produce repeatable material results and exact reason codes. A model may express those reasons or suggest a separate narrow counter, which the engine must evaluate again.

Keep this proposed envelope deliberately small: it is a finite authored scenario case system supporting the reviewed first roster, not a general transport/farm simulator or silent new authority regime.

### Farmer cases that must exist

- **Recorded situation:** name the fixture's operator and actual landowner separately; define whether they are the same actor and the exact negotiation authority. Identify affected plot IDs, the recorded working-site footprint and an authored access-option ID. A broad farmland effect in the physical engine remains distinct from these authored operating facts.
- **Agree:** a stand on an authored disturbed-area option preserves recorded working-field access. The actor accepts the defined operating arrangement, within their delegation. Physical candidate costs/effects are real engine values; no fictional yield or acreage number is inferred.
- **Counter:** the requested option is physically feasible but blocks an authored access connection. The actor offers the explicit alternative access/footprint option and names its concession. Requested preview remains visible; the alternative must be separately evaluated and confirmed.
- **Decline:** an option consumes the recorded working-site footprint or removes the only authored access; the actor declines sponsorship/consent within their own authority. This is distinct from engine infeasibility and from a universal veto on the borough's council decision.
- **Authority negative:** change operator from owner to tenant without title/owner delegation. They can discuss operating access and endorse/oppose, but cannot approve a land sale. All other facts equal, their authority changes without pretending their concern vanished.

### Campus distributor cases that must exist

- **Recorded situation:** use an explicitly authored campus-adjacent fixture at a named existing-site geometry such as `campus_s`, with separate operator and college roles. This does not replace scheduled student housing in ordinary saves or establish college ownership of that parcel. Record an identified campus pedestrian conflict as an authored fact, not a measured collision estimate.
- **Freight-only / crossing conflict:** no customer sales and no consumption. College concern codes address route/crossing, loading window or screening only. The word “beer” does not create storefront alcohol-outlet or on-site-drinking facts.
- **Freight route changed:** a trusted non-campus route option removes the authored crossing conflict. Re-evaluation removes that specific concern and can change stance according to the authored preference rule. Financial/collision impacts remain unknown unless a supported priced operation supplies them.
- **Public sales added:** a separate confirmed option turns on public sales. It introduces the college's explicitly fictional student-facing exposure concern and its actionable concession. It does not imply consumption on site.
- **Consumption proposed:** represent a separate requested operation; if unsupported in the first capability vocabulary, mark it unsupported/clarify rather than treating distributor approval as a bar license. If an authored comparison fixture permits discussion, the character may decline endorsement without acquiring a legal veto.
- **Operator counter:** the developer/operator may reject or counter a feasible loading-window/route request for an explicit authored operational constraint. It cannot grant campus land access, waive a state permit, promise no cost, or rewrite college authority.
