# Dev planning-loop play-test

Use <https://matthnj15-pixel.github.io/Master-Plan/dev/> and a disposable Dev
town. The displayed build revision must match the handoff evidence. Stable has
its own save and leaves composed planning off.

1. In the game menu, download Qwen 1.5B on a compatible WebGPU device and wait
   for model-ready status. The configured models are Qwen2.5 1.5B/3B
   `q4f16_1-MLC`, using WebLLM 0.2.87. Successful caching, generation and usable
   interpretation are separate checks. Quick component controls work when AI
   is unavailable.
2. Bring your own idea. Choose exact plots, then type an unfamiliar project
   name and two or three activities with useful quantities. Check the boundary,
   distinct activities, capacity, assumptions, costs and support. Occupied plots
   must show their modeled demolition/displacement consequences. Drafting must
   leave the public agenda and annual initiative unchanged.
3. Make three typed revisions: add an activity, reduce a quantity, then change
   hours or a buffer. Verify unrelated activities and the site survive. Compare
   a private alternative and return to the original baseline. Check the drawing,
   money, capacity and member support together. Any unsupported subset needs
   explicit acknowledgment before posting.
4. In a new campaign, inspect the farm/access and campus/loading proposals.
   Request a feasible change, inspect it before the resident response, and try
   agree, counter and refuse outcomes. Confirm a concession explicitly. The
   campus stakeholder's concerns must remain distinct from the operator's
   authority. Old towns retain their existing facts rather than acquiring an
   invented historical lease or delegation.
5. Put a private plan on the agenda once. Choose cash or a bond explicitly,
   pitch a displayed fact to a relevant council member, and try a false claim.
   Check factual corrections and the actual saved support change. Favors and
   earmarks are separate actions. Call the real vote; a failed motion is a real
   outcome, not a reason to force the result.
6. End the year, compare the approved drawing with the built town and project
   record, and reload. Follow the ordinary campaign through the first election
   and a later opportunity. Check actual costs, upkeep, commitments and causes.
7. On the physical Duo, force-quit the cached Home Screen app, enable airplane
   mode and reopen it. Repeat a typed draft/revision, decision, year resolution
   and reload. Record device/browser, exact model, cold/warm latency, failures
   and any browser reload. Compare 3B only if it genuinely loads. On failure,
   keep the input and export the selected incident dates from Offline AI →
   Diagnostics, recording the displayed timezone.

The current execution environment lacks `shader-f16` and has no physical Duo.
Its mock-browser and source-audit results do not establish live/offline device
acceptance. Active-space adaptive reuse inside an existing building is outside
the current model; preservation retains assets outside the active allocations.
