## Smallest composition shape and contract follow-through

Use C2's existing `program {version,title,intent,components,conditions,assumptions,omissions}`. A component has stable ID, trusted capability, identity label, quantity/unit, area in plot equivalents and optional `sharedWith`. Conditions are typed modeled hours/deliveries/access/buffer/preserve/affordability. Exact plots belong to the proposal/host, not model output. Keep one engine-owned capability table and one optional trusted program-template table; templates combine primitives through the ordinary pipeline. Do not build project-specific parsers, renderers or transaction routes.

Engine `previewProgram`/`reviseProgram` produces the authoritative candidate plus `programRevision`, deterministic layout, demolition/retention accounting and forecast. UI, actor evaluation, council claims, commit, pending/built records and reload consume that candidate. Forecast should expose cost/range/upkeep, physical quantities, funding, per-member lean/tally and causes; additional project quantities remain outside the nine meters. Schema/design units and fictional coefficients belong in capability definitions and the evidence notes.

The coordinator should resolve these C2 details through small executable probes and explicit contract notes. They are acceptance risks within the authorized work, not new product questions:

1. **Area and retained land:** sum primary occupied area, extra buffer/access allocation and preserved land against usable plots. Shared activities consume a named primary's bounded floor/time budget; prevent cycles, multiple counting and aggregate overbooking. Specify hours/quantity units, delivery enums and bounds. Policy-only programs remain no-site. Engine allocations identify exactly which existing assets/plots are retained; a preservation quantity alone must not imply retention of a particular building.
2. **Revision identity:** fingerprint ordered components/plots, conditions, assumptions/omissions and acknowledged scope; bind authorization, proposal/member/competition identity and full command body. Names retain identity but do not create numerical effects. Re-preview after meaningful state changes. Never approve a stale support/funding display.
3. **Authority at commit:** `confirmed:true` is not proof of authorization. Re-evaluate controller, trusted rights, prerequisites and accepted counter inside `applyCommand`; direct forged calls must fail. Existing nonborough acquisition/backlash under MP-035 must not become a universal owner veto. New farm/access rights must be explicit scenario facts with an attainable resolution.
4. **Complete command coverage:** C2's five command types must preserve every frozen explicit action or document its successor route. Cover table/reject/grant, tax, explicit favors/earmarks, election continuation, new game/import, dev controls, narrative/autosave and account replacement. Keep cash/bond explicit: the current UI's automatic cash-to-bond substitution is unsuitable at a composed confirmation boundary.
5. **Replay and storage:** retain original result, fingerprint, subjects and high-water atomically with game state/initiative/resources. Exact replay after reload precedes live session checks and returns current state unchanged; changed-body or pruned IDs reject. Fresh storage generation, game identity and activation must prevent old actions against replacement towns even if revision numbers coincide.
6. **Fence amendment:** C2 names Dev `masterplan.dev.v4.save`; frozen C named `masterplan.dev.save.v4`. Record the rename and account/Stable/workspace mapping explicitly. Probe recognized predecessor namespaces and protect existing bytes; do not create two independent authoritative v4 branches. Fence parsing runs even with `compositionPlans` off. Unknown/corrupt/newer protected data is read-only. Preserve exact legacy backup bytes/hash.
7. **Reaction/provider details:** C2 requires a committed receipt but its shorthand `run` signature does not show that parameter. Name it explicitly, validate member/action type/fingerprint/retention, and never rescore on reaction/retry. Define typed claims and safe rule/result codes. New diagnostic vocabulary requires an assigned allowlist change, not raw validator text.
