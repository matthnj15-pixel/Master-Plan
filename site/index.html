02 / The Old Mill

One site.
Two futures.

The Old Mill lot comes up for debate in year two. The borough planner wants a memorial park. The merchants want a commercial center. Approve one and the other comes off the agenda.

Compare the proposals

Dana Ruiz / Borough planner

Old Mill Memorial Park

Preserve the memorial and open the riverfront to pedestrians. You gain a place for people to gather and a more walkable town. The borough takes on the cost of creating and maintaining a public park.

Walt Kessler / Merchants’ representative

Old Mill Commons

Turn the lot into a commercial center that adds jobs and shops. A petition with 800 signatures makes the merchants’ case. Building the Commons rules out a park on this site.

In the game, council decides whether either proposal moves forward.
